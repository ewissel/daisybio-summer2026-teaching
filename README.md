# DAISY BIO Summer 2026 Teaching Materials


This is a repo which holds the template code for the DAISY Bio Summer 2026 cohort from the Yale-ESUMS partnership. This material does not including the weekly lectures, practices, or career session materials. Students will need to contact their mentor if the have lost access to the other materials.

Students should mainly look at the jupyter notebook in "scripts/coding_example_doc.ipynb".

This example document is meant to replicate some findings from a previously published study on the zebrafish microbiome, titled "Clearing the Air on Pollutant Disruptions of the Gut–Brain Axis: Developmental Exposure to Benzo[a]pyrene Disturbs Zebrafish Behavior and the Gut Microbiome in Adults and Subsequent Generations" [(doi: https://doi.org/10.3390/toxics13010010)](https://www.mdpi.com/2305-6304/13/1/10). Note that the publication used more nuanced statistical approaches, and for the sake of our learning in DAISY BIO, we are going to opt for methods which are more common in microbiome research in general and mathematically less complicated to allow for easier interpretation. 

Thank you to the Sharpton lab for providing the data and some initial consultation on the study. We love open science!
